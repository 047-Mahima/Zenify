Anxiety & Stress

Box Breathing

Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 4, exhale for 4, hold for 4. Repeat 4 times. Activates your parasympathetic nervous system to reduce immediate anxiety.

Self-help Breathing
5-4-3-2-1 Grounding

Notice 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, and 1 you taste. Brings you back to the present moment during a stress spiral.

Grounding Mindfulness

Mindfulness & Meditation

Body Scan Meditation

Lie down comfortably and slowly direct your awareness from your feet to the top of your head, releasing tension in each area as you go. Takes 10–20 minutes.

Self-Care Toolkit

Sleep Hygiene

Go to bed and wake at consistent times. Avoid screens 30 minutes before sleep. Keep your bedroom cool, dark, and quiet. Poor sleep amplifies every mental health challenge.

Sleep Lifestyle
Physical Movement

Even a 20-minute walk increases serotonin and endorphins. You don't need a gym — consistent moderate movement is what matters most for mood.

Exercise Lifestyle
Social Connection

Isolation worsens mental health. Reach out to one person today — a text, a call, or a coffee. Vulnerability builds real connection.
